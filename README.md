InsightAI is an AI-powered Business Intelligence platform that enables users to analyze business data through natural language instead of traditional Business Intelligence tools.
Rather than requiring users to understand SQL, DAX, Power BI, Tableau, Excel formulas, or database design, InsightAI allows users to upload one or multiple spreadsheets and interact with their data as if they were speaking to a professional data analyst.
The platform automatically understands uploaded datasets, identifies relationships between files, generates meaningful dashboards, answers business questions, detects anomalies, summarizes trends, and provides actionable recommendations.
